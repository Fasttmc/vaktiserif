Istanbul Tea: Color Sort – Privacy Policy

Developer: FAST TMC LLC  |  Last updated: November 15, 2026

Istanbul Tea: Color Sort ("the game") is an offline puzzle game. We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

The game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, settings) is saved only on your own device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
